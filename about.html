ABOUT

What is this?

HELL INTERFACING is a collective of internet musicians who spontaneously decided to form this group to catalog all of our work under one proper netlabel which is ours and ours only.

How did this come to be?

Inspired by the now-defunct Mind Waltz cofounded by our member Vivian Gee, the idea was to have an overarching umbrella term for our releases in an organized order.

What does the future hold?

It's hard to tell as we are not approaching this very seriously as a proper label would be managed. There are ambitious plans we are daydreaming about, and we're actively allocating more attention and resources to this project. That being said, we have something label-related that we've started working on, and I'm personally very excited to see how it turns out.

Is the name a Boards of Canada reference?

Yes.

Why is this site so dogshit?

I don't know how to do soywebdev and I deeply despise J*vaScript.

Do you expect anyone to actively care?

Not necessarily. It'd be nice, but with the way things are in the netscapes, with everyone wanting attention and relevance, one must practice doing things primarily for themselves. That's our philosophy with this label.

Thank you for stopping by, and I hope you found what we do interesting (or deeply naive!)
~nick
